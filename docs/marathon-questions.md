# Marathon organizers: questions

For a first conversation with race organizers. The answers decide what VENTIQ needs to add for races.

**Organizer / race:** ____________________  **Date of race:** ________  **Where:** ________________

## The race
1. How many runners do you expect in total? Last year?
2. Which distances or categories (e.g. 5 km fun run, 10 km, 21 km)? Price and limit for each?
3. Are there age or gender categories with prizes? How do you decide a runner's category?
4. Is there a kids' race or walk?

## Signing up
5. What do runners fill in today, and where (paper, Google Form, WhatsApp)? Can we see the form?
6. Which details are a must: T-shirt size, date of birth, gender, ID number, club, emergency contact, medical conditions, other?
7. Do runners accept a waiver or indemnity? Can we have the wording?
8. Do clubs, schools or companies enter teams? Who pays, and do you need each runner's details?
9. When does entry close? Do you take late entries on the day?

## Paying
10. How do runners pay today (EcoCash, M-Pesa, bank, cash)? Into whose account?
11. Do you give refunds or let injured runners defer to next year? Can an entry be passed to someone else?
12. Do you sell extras (T-shirts, medals, photos, transport)?

## Race day
13. How do runners collect their race pack and bib? When and where?
14. How are bib numbers given out: before race day, or at collection? Any numbering rules per distance?
15. Who does the timing? A timing company with chips, or by hand? Could they take a list of runners from VENTIQ (spreadsheet)?
16. Do you publish results? Where?

## The route
17. Do you have the route as a file (GPX from Strava or Garmin) or a drawn map?
18. What should runners see on it: start, finish, water points, first aid, parking, toilets?

## Communication
19. How do you reach runners today (WhatsApp groups, SMS, email)? What messages do you send before race day?
20. What's the hardest part of running registration today?
